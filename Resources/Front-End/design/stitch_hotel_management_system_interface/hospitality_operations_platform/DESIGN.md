## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-tier architectural aesthetic tailored for boutique and luxury hotel operators, general managers, and front-desk concierges. The interface balances high-density operational utility with the quiet poise of luxury hospitality.

### Design Principles
- **Concierge Precision:** Dense data environments must never feel chaotic. White-glove service standards are translated into generous structural spacing, calm hierarchical contrasts, and deliberate typographic rhythm.
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Replace sterile enterprise monochrome with warm architectural neutrals reminiscent of fine limestone, brushed brass, and midnight velvet.
- **Status Transparency:** Critical room inventories, guest statuses, and housekeeping schedules are readable at a glance without loud, jarring alerts. Color communicates operational urgency with refined sophistication.
- **Architectural Hybrid:** Blends modern minimalism with functional glassmorphism—crisp 1px hairpins, subtle frosted toolbars, and structured grid lines grounding soft, spacious cards.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from classic grand hotels: midnight tailored wool, warm parchment stationery, antique brass accents, and serene botanical greens.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0F172A` - Deep Slate Navy):** The anchor for primary navigation, critical interactive states, headers, and authoritative typography. Represents reliability, timeless structure, and operational focus.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Muted Brass Amber):** Reserved for VIP guest tiers, concierge flags, priority pending bookings, and premium upsell touchpoints.
- **Tertiary (`#059669` - Botanical Emerald):** Operational confirmation, clean/inspected room statuses, and guaranteed arrival confirmations.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FDFCFA` - Warm Ivory Cream):** The underlying foundation that softens screen fatigue during 10-hour shifts, paired with structured container surface neutrals:
  - Surface Pure: `#FFFFFF`
  - Surface Subdued: `#F8F6F0`
  - Border Hairline: `#E7E2D8`
  - Border Focus: `#0F172A`
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - Occupied / Out of Order / Urgent: Coral Crimson (`#E11D48`)
  - Guaranteed / Inspected / Available: Botanical Emerald (`#059669`)
  - Pending / Extended Stay / VIP: Warm Amber (`#D97706`)
  - Reserved / Cleaning in Progress: Soft Slate (`#64748B`)

## Typography

Typographic execution relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its geometric precision, contemporary warmth, and outstanding clarity at compact data-table densities.

### Rules for Application
- **Tabular Figures:** Always apply `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` for currency values, room rates (ADR/RevPAR), check-in times, and folio balances to ensure columnar alignment.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Use `label-sm` and `label-md` in uppercase for status metadata, room types (`DELUXE KING`, `PENTHOUSE SUITE`), and property sections.
- **Hierarchy:** Reserve heavy weights (`600` and `700`) for headers, actionable primary triggers, and monetary totals. Body copy stays at `400` weight to maintain breathable editorial air.

## Layout & Spacing

The PMS interface relies on a flexible, data-optimized layout structured around high information accessibility.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column or 16-column operational fluid grid with a permanent 260px collapsible command sidebar. Gutters sit at `1.5rem` (`gutter-desktop`) with a safe canvas margin of `2rem` (`margin-desktop`).
- **Tablet / Front-Desk iPad (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Sidebar tucks into a persistent slim icon-rail. Margin narrows to `1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** Single-column stacked stream. Off-canvas bottom sheets handle quick guest search, room reassignment, and status changes. Outer margin is `1rem`.

### Spacing Principles
- Density switches allow toggling between **Audit Mode** (compact rows, `space-xs` padding) and **Concierge Mode** (spacious folio cards, `space-lg` padding).
- Layout spacing separates macro view modules (Tape Chart, Room Matrix, Financial Dashboard) with `space-xl` gaps, while metadata pairs within cells maintain tight `space-xs` to `space-sm` unity.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of tailored borders, crisp hairline offsets, and subtle translucent materials.

### Tonal Tiers
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** `#FDFCFA` background. Flat, anchored.
- **Surface Cards (Level 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` enclosed by a 1px border of `#E7E2D8`. Accompanied by an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Active / Floating Overlays (Level 2):** Quick-booking modals, room inspection drawers, and dropdown toolbars. Surface uses translucent ivory glass: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with a `16px` backdrop-blur filter, bounded by `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` border. Shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Command Overlays & Modals (Level 3):** Folio splits, guest check-in wizards, and Night Audit flows. Supported by a deep backdrop: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)` with `8px` backdrop-blur and a sharp floating card shadow: `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design uses deliberate shape hierarchy to differentiate between structural workspaces and micro interactive controls.

### Radius Logic
- **Cards & Primary Modules:** `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`) provides a calm, crafted frame for guest folios, metric cards, and room overview clusters.
- **Form Controls & Table Cells:** Inputs, selects, table rows, and secondary action wrappers use `rounded` (`0.5rem`) to preserve strict spatial efficiency and modern structure.
- **Status Badges & Pill Indicators:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) denote live room states, guest loyalty tags, and dietary badges.
- **Table Data Grids:** Outer bounds retain `rounded-xl` contours, while inner table rows maintain crisp, right-angled architectural dividers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Slate Navy (`#0F172A`) fill, crisp white text, `rounded` (0.5rem) corners, 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Interactive hover shifts to `#1E293B`.
- **VIP / Upgrade Trigger:** Subtle Amber Brass gradient or border with warm ivory text and gold iconography.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** `#FFFFFF` fill with `#E7E2D8` border, Slate Navy text, elevating subtly on hover with `#F8F6F0`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, slate text, active state shows a 10% slate tint.

### Status Indicators & Badges
- **Available / Clean:** Soft mint background (`#ECFDF5`), bold emerald text (`#047857`), 1px border (`#A7F3D0`).
- **VIP / High Priority:** Warm cream-amber background (`#FFFBEB`), deep brass text (`#B45309`), 1px border (`#FDE68A`).
- **Occupied / Clean Due:** Light coral background (`#FFF1F2`), rich rose-red text (`#BE123C`), 1px border (`#FECDD3`).
- **Maintenance / Out of Order:** Slate haze background (`#F1F5F9`), slate text (`#475569`), 1px border (`#CBD5E1`).

### Data Tables (The Tape Chart / Room Matrix)
- **Header:** Sticky positioning, subtle glass background (`rgba(253, 252, 250, 0.95)` with backdrop filter), uppercase `label-md` tracking, fine bottom border `#E7E2D8`.
- **Row Architecture:** Alternate white and `#FAF8F3` (warm tinted zebra), 48px height for audit density, expanding to 64px for check-in operations.
- **Cell Divider:** 1px vertical borders using `#F1EDE4` for strict column alignment across reservation blocks.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Concierge Quick-Search:** Frosted container with left-aligned search icon, keyboard shortcut badge (`⌘K`), `#FFFFFF` background, and fine `#D8D2C5` border.
- **Focus States:** 1.5px stroke using Deep Slate Navy (`#0F172A`) without oversized chromatic rings; subtle outline offset keeps inputs elegant.

### Folio Cards
- High-contrast white surfaces, `rounded-xl` corners, 1px `#E7E2D8` borders, sectioned with horizontal hairline dividers. Financial totals emphasize tabular numerals in bold primary Navy.
